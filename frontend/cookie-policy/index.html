Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device by web servers. They allow websites to remember user preferences, maintain session state, and calculate aggregated traffic metrics.

Categories of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: Strictly required for basic website operation, security defense against spam, and remembering your cookie consent choice.
Performance & Analytics Cookies: Anonymized telemetry cookies (e.g. GA4) that measure page load times, scroll depth, and interaction counts to improve website performance.

Managing Cookie Preferences

You can adjust or revoke your cookie choices at any time via your browser settings or by clearing your local storage cache.
