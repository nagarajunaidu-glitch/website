Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect

Digit Maxz collects information that you directly provide when submitting an inquiry form, booking a strategy consultation, or requesting a quote. This includes your name, work email address, telephone/WhatsApp contact, business name, existing website URL, and project specifications.

We process your data strictly to review your project scope, prepare engineering and marketing feasibility estimates, communicate project roadmaps, and maintain client relationship records. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Our website utilizes UTM parameters and standard analytics to assess campaign performance and user traffic sources. This data is anonymized and used solely to optimize digital user experience and service delivery.

4. Security Safeguards

We employ modern encryption standards (TLS/HTTPS), secure database hosting, and input sanitization protocols to protect your submitted project requirements from unauthorized interception or loss.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data from our records at any time by contacting us at privacy@digitmaxz.com.
