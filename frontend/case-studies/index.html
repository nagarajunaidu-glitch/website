B2B SaaS Architecture

Enterprise Demo Lead Funnel & Sub-1s Re-Engineering

Vertical: Cloud Infrastructure Software • Scope: Web App Dev + Lead Gen + AEO

The Challenge

The client's legacy WordPress portal suffered from 4.2-second initial page load times, complex form abandonment rates exceeding 78%, and zero visibility in modern generative search answers for their software niche.

The Engineering Strategy

Re-architected the frontend using a lightweight static generation framework deployed on global edge CDNs. Replaced single-page friction forms with progressive 2-step qualification modals and integrated JSON-LD SoftwareApplication schemas.

Implementation & Key Learnings

Reducing time-to-interactive from 4.2s down to 0.78s led to a direct 44% lift in form completions. Progressive disclosure in lead forms reduced bounce rate by pre-committing users on initial non-intrusive fields.

Healthcare & Local SEO

Regional Clinic Network Google 3-Pack & Patient Portal

Vertical: Medical & Healthcare • Scope: Local SEO + Custom Web Portal + Google Ads

The Challenge

A multi-location healthcare network was losing patient inquiries to hospital chains because their individual clinic listings had inconsistent phone numbers, poor review aggregation, and no mobile-optimized booking flow.

The Solution

Consolidated NAP citation data across 45 regional directories. Built dedicated, geo-tagged clinic landing pages with MedicalClinic schema and integrated a frictionless 1-click mobile appointment booking system.

Key Learnings

Local intent searchers on mobile devices require immediate direct action buttons (Click-to-Call and WhatsApp Booking). Adding persistent mobile action bars increased same-day patient consultation bookings significantly.
